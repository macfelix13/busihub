Add staff management (invite/roles/deactivate) and business audit log

Owner/manager can invite colleagues by email, assign one of the six
built-in roles, change roles later, and deactivate/reactivate accounts
-- the users.manage/roles.manage/audit.view permissions have existed
since the earliest schema but had no UI until now. Adds an escalation
guard (granting Owner requires business.manage, not just users.manage)
and a lockout guard (can't act on your own row, can't leave the
business with zero active Owners), both covered by a new
tests/security/staff_management.sql. Also enforces profiles.status at
the (app) layout, the same gap businesses.status had before the
Super Admin phase -- a deactivated colleague can no longer sign in.
